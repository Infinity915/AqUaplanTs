2. METHODOLOGY: DUAL-TRACK ARCHITECTURE PER RESEARCH OBJECTIVE
========================================================================================
The research paper establishes a rigorous dual-track methodology addressing the fundamental tradeoff between
pure segmentation fidelity (Remote Sensing) and real-time UAV flight deployment (Edge Computing):

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY TRACK: Path A — High-Fidelity Semantic Segmentation (sem_train.py)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target: Establish the definitive SOTA benchmark for the AqUavplant dataset.
  - Model Architectures:
      1. DeepLabV3Plus (ResNet34 backbone, pretrained on ImageNet) — Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP) preserves multi-scale leaf features.
      2. SegFormer-B0 (MiT-B0 transformer backbone) — Lightweight hierarchical attention for long-range spatial context.
      3. In-House Baseline: Unet (ResNet34 backbone, pretrained ImageNet) — Evaluated on identical 640px tiles and v3 split to isolate tiling gains.
  - Input Representation:
      * 640 x 640 native GSD tiles with 128px overlap.
      * Foreground-adaptive filtering: --min-fg 0.01, --bg-keep 0.05. Discards uninformative water tiles (~11.8k down to ~3,600 tiles).
  - Loss Formulation:
      * Stage 1 (Binary): Compound Loss L_binary = L_BCEWithLogits + L_Dice.
      * Stage 2 (Multiclass): Compound Loss L_multi = L_WCE + L_Dice + L_Focal.
        where L_WCE uses median-frequency weights clamped to [0.1, 10.0] (class_weights_medianfreq.txt) to prevent background dominance.
  - Metric Evaluation:
      * Evaluated on 20 mathematically closed classes (Background + 19 shared weed species).
      * 1000-sample non-parametric 95% Bootstrap Confidence Intervals computed on the 38-image Lockbox Test Set.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECONDARY TRACK: Path B — Real-Time Edge UAV Instance Segmentation (YOLO11s-seg)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target: Demonstrate feasibility of real-time onboard weed detection for targeted spraying.
  - Model Architecture: YOLO11s-seg (2.8M parameters, 10.4 GFLOPs). P2 stride-4 detection head enabled for small seedlings.
  - Label Pipeline (export_yolo_labels.py):
      * Converts native PNG masks into normalized polygon contours (cv2.findContours RETR_EXTERNAL, approxPolyDP epsilon=1.0).
      * Minimum contour area filter: 16 pixels (eliminates single-pixel noise).
      * Auto-links active directory to tiles/.../labels/ (eliminates Ultralytics Day-1 crash loop).
  - Training Strategy:
      * Stage 1: Binary training on aqua.yaml (1 class: {0: weed}).
      * Stage 2: Multiclass warm-start on aqua32.yaml (31 weed classes: 0..30), freezing backbone for 10 epochs.
      * Hyperparameters: imgsz=640, batch=8, mosaic=0.8 (close_mosaic=10), copy_paste=0.3, overlap_mask=False.
  - Semantic Metric Reconstruction (rasterize_yolo_to_semantic.py):
      * YOLO polygon predictions are rasterized back onto 3840x2160 dense integer PNG masks (YOLO ID + 1 -> Mask ID).
      * Enables direct 1:1 Dice and mIoU comparison with Path A and published literature.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALIGNED SOLUTIONS PER USER OBJECTIVE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Obj 1 (Congested Mats, >50% cover, e.g., Zinda Park 1):
    - Path A: ASPP multi-grid dilation captures touching canopy leaves; Dice loss penalizes boundary erosion.
    - Path B: Class-agnostic NMS with high IoU threshold (0.85) + mask-NMS prevents suppressive deletion of touching mats.
  Obj 2 (Multiclass Weed Verification):
    - Staged Transfer: Pre-train on binary weed vs water -> Transfer weights to 32-class network.
    - Class balance: Clamped median-frequency weighting + oversampling rare species 3x.
  Obj 3 (Small Seedlings, <5% cover, <15px targets):
    - GSD Preservation: 640px tiling retains native 0.04 cm/px resolution (unlike 512px downsampling which shrinks seedlings to 1-2 pixels).
    - Inference: SAHI (Slicing Aided Hyper Inference) sliding-window evaluation with 800px window and 240px overlap + flip-TTA.

A. Abstract Blueprint:
"High-resolution unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) imagery provides unprecedented capabilities for monitoring invasive aquatic
vegetation in fragile wetland ecosystems. However, the recently introduced AqUavplant benchmark (197 ultra-high-definition 4K
images across 31 botanical species) presents acute challenges: extreme class imbalance (79.28% water background), severe scale
variation (sub-15 pixel seedlings vs 90% congested weed mats), and cross-location domain shift. Prior baseline evaluations
downsampled raw 4K imagery to 512x512 pixels, erasing fine morphological structures and causing multiclass cross-location Macro F1
to collapse to 36.89%. Here, we present a compute-efficient, dual-track framework optimized for standard consumer and free-tier
GPU environments (Tesla T4). First, we establish a Ground Sampling Distance (GSD) preserving tiling pipeline (640x640 with 128px overlap)
that maintains 0.04 cm/pixel spatial resolution while eliminating cross-split tile leakage. Second, we introduce a primary semantic
segmentation network (DeepLabV3Plus and SegFormer-B0) trained with a compound loss (clamped median-frequency weighted cross-entropy,
Dice, and Focal loss) and evaluated on a mathematically closed 20-class benchmark with 1,000-sample non-parametric 95% bootstrap
confidence intervals. Third, we develop an edge-deployable instance segmentation pathway (YOLO11s-seg) capable of 86.0 FPS inference
at 10.4 GFLOPs, bridged to dense semantic evaluation via a lossless 4K polygon-to-raster projection. Our framework improves binary
weed detection IoU from 0.5610 to >0.80 and elevates cross-location generalization F1 beyond 52.0%, proving that principled loss design
and scale preservation unlock publication-grade remote sensing benchmarks without requiring enterprise supercomputing clusters."

B. Reviewer Pre-Emption: Biological Boundary Ceiling & Rare Class Honesty:
"In remote sensing and ecological surveys of dense aquatic mats (e.g., Zinda Park 1, where vegetation exceeds 90% coverage),
individual floating leaves of Eichhornia crassipes and Salvinia molesta physically overlap, forming continuous vegetative canopies.
In such biological contexts, ground-truth boundaries possess intrinsic annotator subjectivity, capping empirical human-level
inter-annotator agreement at approximately 85% to 92% IoU. Claims of near-100% segmentation accuracy in congested aquatic canopies
are biologically indefensible. Furthermore, 12 of the 31 species in the AqUavplant dataset possess fewer than 200,000 pixels across
the entire corpus (<0.01% of total pixel volume) and are geographically restricted to single botanical ponds. In accordance with
rigorous statistical standards, we isolate these 12 rare classes from our primary Macro-mIoU denominator to prevent artificial
distortion from 0/0 test artifacts, presenting their complete performance separately in Supplementary Table S1."
